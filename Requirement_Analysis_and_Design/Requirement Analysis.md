1. Functional requirements:
    1. Able to transfer water between a water source to the ice maker
    2. Able to detect when a water spillage occurs and notify the user
    3. Ice cub tray is consistantly full
    4. Able to tell the user when specific componenets need to be changed(like filters, pumps, etc).

2. Non functional requirements:
    1. Chosen materials that might be in contact with water must not affect the quality of the ice produced
    2. Electronics must be positioned in places that are easy to reach if they are broken or not porperly functional
    3. In the case of a spill, the system must send a message to the user and shut down all circuitry with 1.5 secounds
    4. from the water source to the ice maker, it should feed filtered water at a constant rate of 
    (x ml/s) until it reaches a set water level of (y L) in the ice maker before stopping. 
    5. The functionality of the filters, pumps, and other internal components are to be expressed on a software in terms of a colour scale where red means "need changing" and green means "good to use"

3. Constraints:
    1. Ice is generated by a pre-bought ice maker(purpose is not to create an ice maker, more so facilitate its output)
    2. It must be able to fit inside of a (give demensions later) drawer
    3. This project must be done within 2 weeks
